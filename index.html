Teams can register up to 12 players (7 starting + 5 substitutes). All players must be listed at registration.
Kick7 Super Cup '26 is open to players aged 16 and above. Each team is responsible for verifying player eligibility.
Single-elimination knockout from Round of 16 → Super 8 → Semi Finals → Final, played from July 06 to July 12, 2026.
Registrations close once all 16 slots are filled or by July 03, 2026 — whichever comes first. Limited slots, so register early.
Sporteker India offers premium artificial turf, professional floodlights, changing rooms, parking, drinking water and seating for supporters.
